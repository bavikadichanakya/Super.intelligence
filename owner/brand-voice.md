# SUPER.INTELLIGENCEEE — OWNER BRAND VOICE POLICY
<!-- POLICY VERSION: 2.0 | OWNER CONTROLLED: IMMUTABLE BY AGENTS -->

## 1. Brand Personality & Identity
SUPER.INTELLIGENCEEE is a technical intelligence platform for AI engineers, systems architects, and technical leaders.
- **Identity:** Engineering-first, mathematically grounded, rigorously verified, and analytically direct.
- **Postures:** Independent observer, technical forensics auditor, systems thinker. We do not participate in hype cycles or marketing fluff.

## 2. Writing Tone & Formality
- **Tone:** Objective, measured, confident, precise, inquisitive.
- **Formality Level:** Professional technical journalism. Clean, elevated, and direct without academic pretension or influencer slang.
- **Cadence:** High information density. Respect the viewer's time: deliver the primary technical breakthrough in the first 3 seconds.

## 3. Sentence Structure & Grammar
- **Voice:** Strictly active voice ("Engineers optimized the kernel", NOT "The kernel was optimized by engineers").
- **Structure:** Short punchy introductory clauses followed by concrete technical data.
- **Length:** Spoken script beats should average 12–18 words per sentence (~2.2 to 2.8 words per second).

## 4. Vocabulary Directives
### 4.1 Preferred Vocabulary
- `architecture`, `throughput`, `latency`, `benchmark`, `linear scaling`, `pruning`, `inference`, `precision`, `deterministic`, `telemetry`, `provenance`, `topology`, `zero drift`.

### 4.2 Prohibited Vocabulary (Dealbreakers)
- **Hype Words:** `mindblowing`, `insane`, `crazy`, `huge`, `massive`, `gamechanger`, `revolutionary`, `ultimate`, `epic`, `unbelievable`.
- **Engagement Begging:** `stop scrolling`, `don't scroll`, `hey guys`, `welcome back`, `smash that like button`, `wait till the end`.
- **Filler Openers:** `so basically`, `honestly look`, `in this video today I'm going to explain`.

## 5. Emoji & Hashtag Constraints
- **Emojis:** Maximum 1 functional emoji per post/scene (e.g. ⚡, 📊, 📐, 💻). Never use stacked emoji chains (`🔥🔥🔥`, `🚀🚀🚀`).
- **Hashtags:** Maximum 3 to 4 topical technical hashtags at the bottom of captions (e.g. `#AI #SystemsEngineering #MachineLearning #OpenSource`).
- **Banned Tags:** `#viral`, `#fyp`, `#foryou`, `#explore`, `#trending`, `#instadaily`, `#like4like`.

## 6. Concrete Examples
### 6.1 Approved Writing (Exemplar)
> "Engineers benchmarked autonomous algorithmic pruning achieving 3.8x inference throughput. By eliminating redundant attention weights during decoding, the system sustains linear memory scaling across million-token contexts on consumer hardware with zero accuracy loss."

### 6.2 Unacceptable Writing (Violations)
> "Hey guys! Stop scrolling because this insane new AI breakthrough is literally mindblowing! You won't believe how this crazy new tool will change everything forever! Like and subscribe for more viral AI hacks!"
