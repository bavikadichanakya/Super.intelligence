## 1. Strategic Brand Positioning
- **Core Mission:** Provide forensic, evidence-grounded, zero-hype technical intelligence on frontier AI architectures and local-first software engineering.
- **Value Proposition:** While mainstream media spreads generalized hype and influencer fluff, SUPER.INTELLIGENCEEE delivers the actual architectural mechanics, empirical benchmarks, and systems implications of deep tech milestones.

### 2.1 Primary Audience
- AI/ML Engineers, Systems Architects, Backend Developers.
- Technical Founders, CTOs, and Engineering Leads.
- Graduate Researchers and Deep Tech Builders.

### 2.2 Excluded Audiences (Do NOT Cater To)
- "AI hustle" / drop-shipping / passive income creators.
- Speculative crypto/meme traders.

## 3. Four Core Editorial Content Pillars
1. **Frontier Architecture:** Mathematical and algorithmic innovations in neural networks (attention mechanisms, state-space models, linear context scaling, algorithmic pruning, post-training quantization).
2. **Local-First & Edge AI:** Running frontier capabilities offline on consumer hardware, zero cloud lock-in, private inference, and local toolchains.
3. **Systems Reliability & Benchmarks:** Forensic performance analysis: real-world tokens/sec, latency profiles, peak VRAM allocations, and hardware bottleneck audits.
4. **Verified Open Source:** Deep dives into open weights, reproducible research papers, open-source developer tooling, and independent benchmark replications.

## 4. Topics to Own
- Million-token linear memory scaling.
- Workstation AI inference optimization.
- Multi-agent orchestration and deterministic governance.
- Zero-cost, local-first production toolchains.

## 5. Evidentiary Standards & Proof Requirements
- **Permissible Sources:** Peer-reviewed papers (ArXiv), verified GitHub source repositories, and official research lab publications (DeepMind, Meta FAIR, Anthropic, OpenAI Research).
- **Prohibited Sources:** Anonymous tweets, unverified Reddit rumors, secondary aggregator rewrites, sponsored promotional press releases without code.
