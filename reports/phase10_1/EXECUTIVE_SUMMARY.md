## 2. Core Defect Repairs & Architectural Innovations

### A. Subtitle Engine Reconstruction (Zero Edge Clipping)
- **Previous Failure:** Subtitle phrases were rendered as single long strings. When exceeding 1080px, horizontal centering placed the first character at a negative offset (`X = -85px`), clipping the opening and closing words off-screen.
- **Phase 10.1 Solution:** The new `AdvancedSubtitleEngine` enforces a strict **900px maximum width** (leaving 90px left/right margins) and automatically balances sentences across **two clean lines**.
- **100% Transparent Backing:** Subtitles have zero black boxes or opaque pills. A dual-pass soft ambient halo (`#FFFFFF`, alpha 220) ensures pristine legibility over both light backgrounds and dark hardware geometry. Active words highlight in real-time in sync with Kokoro phonetic timestamps.

### B. Proper Text Layout Engine (`rendering/text_layout_engine.py`)
- Real FreeType font metrics (`draw.textbbox`) calculate glyph bounds, line leading, and baseline metrics.
- Intelligent line-wrapping protects atomic scientific units (`10 mK`, `840 ns`, `d = 7`, `10^25 YR`, `Lambda = 1.0`).

### C. Zero Text and Graph Collisions
- **Scene 1 (Chandelier):** Dilution refrigerator camera motion bounded strictly below `Y=240`, protected by a gradient mask. Subtitle placed at `Y=1680` (400px clearance below QPU).
- **Scene 2 (Coherence Crisis):** Thermometer scale expanded with 32px padding, ensuring `300 K` label never touches border.
- **Scene 3 (Google Willow):** Qubit grid capacitor radius expanded to 15px with gold wirebond accents.
- **Scene 4 (Stabilizer Dynamics):** Bit-flip and healed badges clamped within bounded safe regions.
- **Scene 5 (Nature Milestone):** X-axis title `SURFACE CODE DISTANCE (d)` separated by 43px below qubit annotations. Y-axis numbers given 80px left padding.
- **Scene 6 (Supremacy Benchmark):** 88px central gutter separates Frontier and Willow columns. Line-leading prevents vertical crowding.
- **Scene 7 (Fault-Tolerant Era):** Node status badges enlarged to 24pt bold emerald pills.
