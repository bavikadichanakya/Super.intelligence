# PROHIBITED CLAIMS & HALLUCINATION BOUNDARIES

1. **Unverified Speedups:** Never claim a performance multiplier (e.g. "10x speedup", "100x efficiency") unless it is explicitly documented in the primary benchmark table or paper abstract.
2. **Artificial General Intelligence Claims:** Never state or imply that any current model has achieved AGI, sentience, or superintelligence.
3. **Hardware Independence:** Never state that large model inference runs on consumer devices without citing specific quantization levels (e.g. 4-bit AWQ/GGUF) or parameter sizes.
4. **Zero-Cost Guarantees:** Do not promise third-party commercial software is permanently free unless licensed under verifiable open-source terms (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD).
5. **Fabricated Benchmarks:** Never synthesize or interpolate benchmark metrics not present in the verified topic dossier.
