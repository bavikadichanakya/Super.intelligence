## 1. REGULATORY & LICENSING COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

To maintain complete commercial safety and intellectual rigor, SUPER.INTELLIGENCEEE operates under a strict **Zero-Cost, 100% Permissive Open Licensing Standard**.

Every visual asset, texture, paper figure, icon, sound effect, and audio sample utilized in automated productions must satisfy one of three legal regimes:
1. **Public Domain / CC0:** Unrestricted commercial and non-commercial reuse without attribution requirement (e.g., NASA, US Government, NIH, Wikimedia Commons CC0).
2. **Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY 4.0):** Permissive commercial reuse with machine-readable metadata attribution embedded in the project manifest and video description.
3. **Procedurally Generated / AI-Native / Clean-Room Synthetic:** Assets generated entirely by code (Pillow, Three.js, NumPy, SVG) from first-principles mathematical specifications.

## 3. PROCEDURAL SYNTHESIS VS THIRD-PARTY ASSET RISKS

While open repositories are valuable, relying on third-party raster images introduces severe risks for an automated 1080x1920 60fps vertical pipeline:
1. **Resolution Degradation:** Static images downloaded from papers are frequently low-resolution (72 DPI, 800x600 px). Scaling them up to 1080x1920 results in blurry, pixelated artifacts.
2. **Aspect Ratio Incompatibility:** Most scientific diagrams are landscape (16:9 or 4:3), leaving awkward empty letterboxes when forced into 9:16 vertical reels.
3. **Licensing Churn:** Third-party hosting URLs frequently break or change license terms unexpectedly.

### The Superior Solution: Procedural Clean-Room Reconstruction
Instead of scraping and displaying raw paper screenshots, our autonomous studio extracts the **underlying mathematical and geometric parameters** from the paper and procedurally synthesizes pristine, vector-sharp 1080x1920 assets:
- We don't screenshot the Willow chip; we reconstruct the 105-qubit lattice with exact pitch and coordinate geometry using NumPy.
- We don't take a grainy photo of a dilution refrigerator; we render the physical stages with multi-stop metallic shaders inspired by verified physical architectures.
- We don't screenshot a blurry threshold plot; we render the mathematical curve $P_L = C \cdot (P / P_{th})^{(d+1)/2}$ directly with glowing vector paths.

This guarantees:
- Infinite resolution (native 1080x1920 at 60 fps).
- 100% intellectual property ownership and zero copyright risk.
- Fully controllable procedural animation of every individual component.
