# PLATFORM DISTRIBUTION GUIDELINES

## 1. Instagram Reels
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080x1920).
- **Target Duration:** 45 to 65 seconds (optimal retention curve).
- **Safe Zone Margin:** Subtitles and text cards must clear bottom 340px margin.
- **Caption Rules:** Hook in first 125 characters (before "more" truncation). 3 to 4 technical hashtags.
- **Publishing Gate:** Affirmative human approval required in `approvals` table.

## 2. YouTube Shorts
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080x1920).
- **Target Duration:** 50 to 60 seconds.
- **Title Character Limit:** Under 60 characters for desktop search, under 40 characters for mobile feed.
- **Packaging:** Title/thumbnail pairing linter must pass without buzzword shouting.
- **Publishing Gate:** Affirmative human approval required in `approvals` table.
