## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 5 overhauls the editorial pipeline to ensure that scripts no longer read like academic abstracts or sterile corporate slides. 

The reference video (`This is what i want.mp4`) demonstrates how to communicate profound technical depth (MoE architecture, C99 inference without BLAS, SSD chunk streaming, determinism across memory tiers) in a conversational, fast-paced, and captivating manner.

### The Five Editorial Tenets of Reference-Style Scripts:
1. **The 3-Second Technical Contrast Hook:** Opens immediately with a stark engineering paradox ("run a 2.78 trillion parameter model on just 8 gigs of RAM and no GPU").
2. **Short, Spoken Sentences:** Sentence lengths strictly bounded to 10–18 words, optimized for breath control and rhythmic delivery.
3. **Conversational American English:** Natural diction with informal signposts ("The trick is...", "And even the always-on part...", "The catch is...", "So, follow for more.").
4. **Concrete Visual/Auditory Complements:** The narration explains the conceptual *why*, while the graphics demonstrate the physical *how* (e.g. while saying "streams in layer by layer", the screen shows slabs peeling off a 93-layer stack).
5. **Seamless Loop Ending:** The final sentence organically leads the viewer back to the opening statement ("proves size alone won't stop a model running on your machine" → "Now you can run...").

## 4. Script-to-Visual Event Synchronization

In traditional videos, text is displayed in static blocks. In the Phase 5 architecture, the script is broken down into **visual trigger events**:

```

"The trick is that Kimi's split into 896 small experts per layer, and for every word, only 16 wake up, so it pulls just those off your drive."

Visual Trigger Pipeline:
[t = 0.0s] Kinetic Text: "that Kimi's split"
[t = 0.6s] Grid Node Array scales in (896 expert squares, gray)
[t = 1.4s] Badge reveals: "LAYER 1 of 93, each with its own set"
[t = 2.0s] Kinetic Text: "and for every word"
[t = 2.8s] Kinetic Text: "only 16 wake up"
[t = 3.0s] 16 specific grid nodes light up bright red; badge updates: "16 awake · 880 asleep"
[t = 4.2s] 3D M.2 NVMe SSD slides into lower canvas with badge [SSD]
[t = 5.0s] Dotted bezier conduits stream packets between SSD chips and active red nodes
[t = 6.2s] Kinetic Text: "off your drive"
```

This tight lock between spoken words and visual animations creates the engaging energy characteristic of the reference benchmark.
