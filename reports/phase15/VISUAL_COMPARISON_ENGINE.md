## 1. Engine Objective & Architectural Role

The **Visual Comparison Engine** is an automated narrative and visual generation system that identifies opportunities to bridge abstract computer science concepts to intuitive physical realities.

Rather than relying on abstract text definitions or complex circuit diagrams, the engine pairs every major technical bottleneck or architecture with a memorable, physicalized, side-by-side visual comparison.

## 3. The 4 Golden Rules of Visual Metaphors

Every comparison generated by the engine must adhere to four strict criteria:

1. **Immediate Intuition ($\le 2$ Seconds):**
   - The visual contrast must be instantly readable without voiceover. A viewer scrolling on mobile should grasp the difference within two seconds.
2. **Direct Structural Mapping:**
   - The metaphor must map 1:1 with real physical phenomena. In the GPU analogy, the workers are doing simple math (painting one color), not solving chess puzzles, directly mirroring the simplified ALU structure of streaming multiprocessors.
3. **Explicit Boundary Clarification:**
   - The script must explicitly state where the metaphor ends: *"Of course, GPU cores aren't tiny painters—they are silicon Arithmetic Logic Units sharing register files and high-bandwidth memory."*
4. **Immediate Transition to Silicon / Code:**
   - The comparison serves as a launchpad, not the destination. Once intuition is established, the scene immediately transitions into the real 3D silicon die or code snippet.
