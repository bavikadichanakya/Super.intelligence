## 2. TIMESTAMPED REFERENCE BREAKDOWN WITH ART-DIRECTION RULES

### Observation 1: Beat 1 (0.00s – 7.20s) — Volumetric Parameter Core
- **Observed Surface:** Obsidian dark `#07090E` with deep blue radial rim light.
- **Hero Object:** Giant 3D voxel cube representing 2.78T parameters, occupying 82% of screen width.
- **Design Rule 1:** When using dark backgrounds, light the hero object with a strong key-light and directional rim light. Never leave the object as a flat 2D silhouette.

### Observation 2: Beat 2 (7.20s – 15.10s) — Architectural Light Document
- **Observed Surface:** Warm studio paper `#F5F3ED` (RGB `[162, 162, 154]` ambient tone).
- **Hero Object:** Minimalist, high-end editorial GitHub repository card with clean drop shadow.
- **Design Rule 2:** The transition to the explanatory body must shift to warm, bright, high-readability surfaces. Use soft black typography (`#1E293B`) on ivory backgrounds.

### Observation 3: Beat 3 (15.10s – 22.40s) — 3D Physical Extruded Columns
- **Observed Surface:** Light architectural grey `#E8E6E0` with soft floor shadow.
- **Hero Object:** Three massive 3D extruded pillar columns rising from the bottom baseline.
- **Design Rule 3:** Benchmark comparisons must be rendered as physical architectural structures with depth, top bevels, side shading, and floor contact shadows—never flat 2D spreadsheet bars.

### Observation 4: Beat 4 & 5 (22.40s – 40.50s) — Layer Slabs & Sparse Expert Dies
- **Observed Surface:** Warm grey studio surface `#E8E6E0`.
- **Hero Object:** Volumetric neural layer slabs physically detaching and streaming along an arc conduit; 896-expert honeycomb grid with active crimson dies.
- **Design Rule 4:** Abstract neural networks must be represented as physical hardware/silicon modules with tangible mass and depth.

### Observation 5: Beat 6 & 7 (40.50s – 58.00s) — Physical Hardware PCB & Split Determinism
- **Observed Surface:** Studio grey surface `#E8E6E0`.
- **Hero Object:** Physical M.2 NVMe SSD PCB with gold PCIe contact fingers and textured NAND chips. Split-screen vertical arena comparing laptop vs cluster output.
- **Design Rule 5:** Technical hardware must look like physical industrial engineering (silicon packaging, gold contact pins, matte substrate). Split-screen comparisons must be balanced cleanly across the vertical centerline.

## 3. SUMMARY OF MANDATED PHASE 7 DESIGN RULES

1. **Light Editorial Surfaces are Default:** Default to `#F5F3ED` (Warm Ivory), `#FAFAF8` (Soft White), or `#E8E6E0` (Architectural Grey).
2. **Hero Objects Must Be Massive:** Occupy 65%–85% of canvas width.
3. **Grounding Shadows are Mandatory:** Every object must cast a soft ambient contact shadow onto the studio floor.
4. **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast Sans Bold headers (48–56 pt) + Georgia Italic emphasis (32–36 pt). No multi-line paragraph text blocks on screen.
5. **Dynamic Storyboard Planning:** Scene count and compositions must follow the physical subject matter—no hardcoded 7-scene dashboard templates.
