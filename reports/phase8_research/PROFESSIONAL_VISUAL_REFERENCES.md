## 3. SYNTHESIZED ARCHITECTURAL TAKEAWAYS FOR PHASE 8

1. **Every single world-class explainer avoids generic box templates.** The visuals are tailor-crafted to the unique physics, geometry, or architecture of the subject.
2. **Camera motion is continuous and purposeful.** 3Blue1Brown, Apple, and Linear never freeze the camera on a slide. The camera is an active explorer moving through spatial coordinates.
3. **Typography must have weight, hierarchy, and active kinetic connection to speech.** 80+ pt editorial sans headers paired with precise metadata chips.
4. **Lighting establishes genre.** Obsidian black for dramatic scale and silicon chips (Apple/Linear); warm ivory for technical architecture, software, and documents (3Blue1Brown/Stripe).
5. **Authentic evidence creates unshakeable credibility.** Real papers, real terminals, and verified benchmark curves turn abstract marketing claims into undeniable truth.
